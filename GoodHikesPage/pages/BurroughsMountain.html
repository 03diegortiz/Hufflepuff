Burroughs Mountain Trail

General Information:

  • Location: Mount Rainier National Park, Paradise Area
  • Difficulty: Hard
  • Trail Type: Out and Back
  • Distance: Approximately 9.0 miles round trip
  • Estimated Time: Unknown
  • Elevation Gain: Approximately 2,500ft, highest point 7,828 feet
  • Parking: Parking pass required WTA Passes
  • Entrance Fee: National Park entrance fee required
  • Pets: Not allowed on trails
  • Scenery: Offer some of the closest and most dramatic vantage points of Mount Rainier and its massive ice fields
