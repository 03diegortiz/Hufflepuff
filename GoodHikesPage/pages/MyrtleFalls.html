Myrtle Falls

General Information:

  • Location: Mount Rainier National Park, Paradise
  • Difficulty: Easy
  • Trail Type: Out and Back
  • Distance: Approximately 0.8 miles round trip
  • Estimated Time: 20–30 minutes
  • Elevation Gain: Approximately 100 feet
  • Parking: Paradise parking lot
  • Entrance Fee: National Park entrance fee required
  • Pets: Not allowed
  • Scenery: Myrtle Falls, Mount Rainier, Edith Creek, and alpine meadows
